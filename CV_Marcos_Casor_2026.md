## Summary

I've been working with .NET since 2011: consultancies, a bank, two oil companies and two products of my own.

Right now I'm at Chevron, writing C#/.NET backend services, Azure Functions for background processing, and the Azure DevOps pipelines that ship them. I also look after the team's Angular apps.

What I like most is taking a system nobody wants to touch and leaving it in a state where the team can work without fear. I moved .NET Framework 4.0 to 4.8 with all the Entity Framework dependencies riding along, and took Xamarin code to MAUI. I also upgraded Angular 12 to 14 on a product of my own. None of that is glamorous. It's the work that keeps a product alive.

I taught for two years at Cervantes: mobile development with Flutter and web programming with .NET Core and React. You have people in front of you asking why, and that forces you to actually understand what you're doing.

I built QuieroCuentos and EnSierrasChicas end to end. That's where I learned deadlines and budgets aren't abstract.

These days I'm putting hours into LLM applications: RAG, agent orchestration, Python. Still learning, and I'd rather say so.
